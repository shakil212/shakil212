## 🔬 Research and Development

### 🌱 EcoToken-BN

**EcoToken-BN: Quantifying and Mitigating the Tokenization, Energy, Cost, and Carbon Tax of Bangla Language Models**

EcoToken-BN is a research initiative focused on investigating how inefficient tokenization affects Bangla language models.

The project aims to measure and mitigate:

* Tokenization overhead
* Computational energy consumption
* Model inference cost
* Carbon emissions
* Processing latency
* Resource inefficiency in Bangla language processing

The broader objective is to support the development of more efficient, affordable, and environmentally sustainable Bangla language technologies.

---

### 🧠 Cognitive State-Driven Generative Scaffolding

**Cognitive State-Driven Generative Scaffolding: Aligning Large Language Models with Deep Knowledge Tracing for Adaptive STEM Tutoring**

This research explores an intelligent STEM tutoring framework that combines **Large Language Models** with **Deep Knowledge Tracing** to provide personalized and cognitively appropriate learning support.

The proposed system aims to:

* Estimate a learner’s current knowledge state
* Identify learning gaps and misconceptions
* Track knowledge development over time
* Generate personalized explanations, hints, and questions
* Adapt the difficulty of learning materials
* Provide step-by-step instructional scaffolding
* Reduce unnecessary assistance and learner dependency
* Improve student engagement and learning outcomes

The main objective is to move beyond general-purpose AI tutoring by enabling an LLM to generate educational support based on a learner’s continuously updated knowledge state.
